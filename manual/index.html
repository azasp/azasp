1. Getting started

Open MyTracker and sign in with the account invited or created for you. Your available navigation is based on the role assigned to your account.

  1. Sign in using the configured authentication method.
  2. Open Profile to review the account details shown to you.
  3. Choose the relevant family member where the screen provides a member selector.
  4. Use the navigation menu to open the features available to your role.
If the app says your account is waiting for approval, ask the family administrator to review your membership and assign the appropriate role. Do not create another account to bypass approval.

3. Overview

The Overview shows a quick summary for the selected family member, including money balance, stars, shopping spend and the latest recorded temperature when available. The member selector lets permitted roles switch between family members. The default member choice is stored in that browser.

4. Money and stars

Money

  1. Open Money and select the relevant member.
  2. Enter a note, amount and date.
  3. Choose Add for money received or Spend for money used.
  4. Review the updated balance and entry history.

Stars

Use Stars to record earned or deducted stars with a reason. The available total is shown on the screen. Eligible roles can use Rewards to redeem a configured reward when enough stars are available.

5. Shopping and rewards

Shopping entries record an item and price. Parent and Admin roles can mark an item as completed using its checkbox. Admin can remove an entry. Rewards are configured in Masters and redeemed from the Rewards area when the member has enough stars.

6. Classes and health

Classes records class, activity or homework notes. Health records health notes and supported measurements such as temperature, weight and height, along with doctor visits and vaccinations. Use clear dates and descriptions. MyTracker is a record-keeping tool, not a medical diagnosis service.

7. Expenses and transactions

The Expenses area summarises income and expenses, displays transactions and import history, and provides CSV import for supported exported data. Parent and Admin roles can use editing/import controls.

  1. Choose the CSV file exported from the supported expense tracker.
  2. Review the detected row count and mapping assumptions.
  3. Import the file and inspect the resulting transactions.
  4. Review categories, dates, amounts and accounts; correct any incorrect classification.
Always review imported data. Do not assume a CSV has been fully reconciled just because the import completed.

8. Credit-card bills and reconciliation

MyTracker separates a bank debit, payment-processor processing and issuer credit. These are distinct events. A payment match within ₹1 tolerance is a candidate match only; it does not itself confirm settlement.

  1. Register the bank account and credit-card account with institution, nickname and last four digits.
  2. Add the card bill with statement amount and due date.
  3. Link the relevant bank debit to the repayment as appropriate.
  4. Record processor acceptance when there is evidence of processing.
  5. Record issuer credit only when the card issuer confirms receipt.
  6. Allocate the repayment to the bill and verify the outstanding amount.
Never mark a bill settled solely because the bank account was debited or a processor says the payment was processed. Settlement depends on issuer credit and the required allocation.

9. Admin: family invitations

Family invitations are managed from Family & devices. The family owner/admin enters the invitee's name and email. The invitation remains pending until the intended invitee authenticates and accepts that specific invitation.

  • Invite the correct email address.
  • The invitee follows the invitation email and signs in with the matching email account.
  • The app accepts the specific invitation identified by its invitation ID.
  • Only that invitation becomes active; another pending invitation for the same email in another family remains pending.

Do not forward invitation links. If the wrong email was invited, resolve the invitation with the administrator rather than attempting to change account identity.

10. Admin: device registration and approval

  1. Open Family & devices and generate a one-time pairing code.
  2. Use the code in the MyTracker iPhone Shortcut to register the device.
  3. The new device appears as pending approval.
  4. Review the device and approve it only if it belongs to the intended family member.
  5. Reject or revoke devices that should not submit imports.

The pairing code expires after 15 minutes and is single-use. Device credentials are private bearer secrets: do not share or paste them into messages or support requests.

11. iPhone SMS imports

The iPhone Shortcut sends messages exposed by the iOS Message automation to MyTracker. The device checks its approval status before import. MyTracker stores parsed event metadata and a message hash for deduplication; the original SMS body is not stored by the import function.

  1. Pair the iPhone using the one-time code.
  2. Wait for an administrator to approve the device.
  3. Run the Shortcut and verify that the device status is approved.
  4. Review import batch status and resulting transactions in Expenses.
  5. Investigate skipped, failed, ambiguous or unlinked events.
iOS does not give this Shortcut unrestricted access to the entire Messages history. Only messages exposed by the configured automation can be sent. Test on the actual iPhone and iOS version.

12. Admin guide

People and roles

Use People to manage the family people shown in the application. Assign only the role required for the person's responsibilities. Pending accounts should not be treated as active family members.

Masters

Masters supplies reusable values such as classes, shopping items, star reasons, rewards, conversion rate, money categories and medicines. Keep names clear and avoid creating duplicate options.

Family and devices

Use this area for invitations, device pairing and approval, and the family/device controls exposed to administrators. Review device ownership before approval and revoke credentials when a device is lost or no longer trusted.

Financial accounts

Register bank and credit-card accounts using a nickname and last four digits. Do not enter full card numbers, PINs, CVVs, internet-banking passwords or OTPs.

13. Privacy and security

  • Use your own account and do not share sign-in links or device credentials.
  • Never enter a full card number, CVV, PIN, bank password or OTP into MyTracker notes.
  • Approve only devices you recognise and revoke lost devices promptly.
  • Share only the family information needed by each role.
  • Use dummy or anonymised information when reporting a problem.
  • Contact the administrator if you see information belonging to another family or person.

14. Troubleshooting

My account is waiting for approval

Ask the family administrator to check the invitation and membership status. Ensure you signed in with the email address that received the invitation.

My device is pending

An administrator must approve it. The Shortcut must stop importing until its status is approved.

SMS import is not showing a transaction

Check the import batch status, device approval, duplicate-message behaviour and event classification. Some card-payment events are stored as repayment events rather than ordinary expenses.

Card bill still shows outstanding

Check whether the bank debit, processor stage, issuer-credit confirmation and repayment allocation have all been recorded. A bank debit alone is not settlement.

Something looks wrong

Record the screen, approximate time and steps that led to the issue. Remove personal data, account identifiers and SMS contents before sharing a screenshot with support.

About this manual

This manual describes the current application navigation and the documented invitation, device and SMS workflows. Screens and permissions can change as MyTracker evolves. If a feature is not visible to your role, ask an administrator rather than assuming it is enabled.
